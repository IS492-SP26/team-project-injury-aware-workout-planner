# CP4 User Experience Survey (SUS-Style, 1-5 Scale)

Use this survey after a participant completes the AdaptFit AI study tasks.

## Descriptive Questions

1. What is your name?
2. Are you a medical professional? If yes, please describe your level of education and practice.
3. Are you a frequent gym-goer? If yes, please describe how often you go and how long you have been going.

## Response Scale

For each statement, select one option:

1. Strongly Disagree  
2. Disagree  
3. Neutral  
4. Agree  
5. Strongly Agree

## Survey Statements


Please rate each statement on a scale of 1 (Strongly disagree) to 5 (Strongly agree):

4. I clearly understood that AdaptFit AI is a support tool and not a replacement for professional medical advice.
5. The app made it clear when I should pause and consult a professional instead of continuing workout modifications.
6. I found the overall workflow — assessment, readiness check, and workout adaptation/results — easy to follow.
7. I was confused about whether this tool is mainly for planning, real-time coaching, or workout logging.
8. The injury assessment questions, including pain, functional screening, and movement limits, felt relevant to my condition or profile.
9. The risk labels and safer substitutions seemed accurate and appropriate for the injured area.
10. The system provided enough explanation for why an exercise was flagged as risky and how the alternative was safer.
11. I would need substantial help from a trainer or clinician to use this system correctly.
12. The adapted output reduced the effort I would normally spend figuring out what to skip or modify in a workout.
13. I trust this tool to support safer mid-stage recovery workouts when used with reasonable caution.


## Optional Follow-Up (Recommended for CP4 Qualitative Analysis)

- What was the single most helpful part of the experience?
- What part felt least reliable or most risky?
- What should be improved first to make this safer and more trustworthy?

## Scoring Note (for analysis team)

- Items **4** and **9** are reverse-scored.
- Higher total scores indicate better perceived usability, clarity, and safety confidence.
