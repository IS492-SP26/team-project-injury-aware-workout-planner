# CP4 User Experience Survey (SUS-Style, 1-5 Scale)

Use this survey after a participant completes the AdaptFit AI study tasks.

## Response Scale

For each statement, select one option:

1. Strongly Disagree  
2. Disagree  
3. Neutral  
4. Agree  
5. Strongly Agree

## Survey Statements

1. I clearly understood that AdaptFit AI is a support tool and not a replacement for professional medical advice.
2. The app made it clear when I should pause and consult a professional instead of continuing workout modifications.
3. I found the overall workflow (assessment -> readiness -> workout adaptation/results) easy to follow.
4. I was confused about whether this tool is mainly for planning, real-time coaching, or workout logging. *(reverse-scored)*
5. The injury assessment questions (pain, functional screening, movement limits) felt relevant to my condition/profile.
6. The readiness outcome (including "not cleared" situations) seemed appropriate for the information I provided.
7. The risk labels and safer substitutions looked accurate and appropriate for the injured area.
8. The system gave enough explanation for why an exercise was flagged as risky and how the alternative was safer.
9. I would need substantial help from a trainer/clinician to use this system correctly. *(reverse-scored)*
10. The adapted output reduced the effort I normally spend figuring out what to skip or modify in a workout.
11. I trust this tool to support safer mid-stage recovery workouts when used with reasonable caution.
12. I would use this tool again for injury-aware workout planning/modification.

## Optional Follow-Up (Recommended for CP4 Qualitative Analysis)

- What was the single most helpful part of the experience?
- What part felt least reliable or most risky?
- What should be improved first to make this safer and more trustworthy?

## Scoring Note (for analysis team)

- Items **4** and **9** are reverse-scored.
- Higher total scores indicate better perceived usability, clarity, and safety confidence.
